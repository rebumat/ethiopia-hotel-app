# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (versionCode 2)
- Saved hotels (favourites): heart button on hotel cards and the hotel detail screen; a new Saved screen (Profile → Saved hotels) with an empty state; saved on the device in SharedPreferences.
- Recent searches: last 5 searches shown on the Explore screen; tap to re-run, Clear to remove them.
- Upcoming trip countdown: a card on the My Bookings screen for the next upcoming trip (days until check-in, hotel, dates, View booking). Shown only when an upcoming booking exists.
- Recently viewed hotels: last 8 hotels opened, shown in a "Recently viewed" row on the Home screen.
- Reviews were already present before 1.1.0 (star rating + comment after checkout, Room-backed, average rating updates) — no change in this release.

## 1.0.0-demo (versionCode 1)
- Initial demo build: hotel booking + hotel management (PMS), 20 fictional hotels, 8 languages, simulated payments, on-device data.
